We build the cars of tomorrow

ENISo Auto Crew brings together electronics, mechanics, simulation and software to design real vehicle projects, from first sketch to working prototype.

Join the crewExplore our projects

Learn by building

We are a student club at the National Engineering School of Sousse. Every member picks a real project, works in a team, and learns the tools used in the automotive industry.

  • Our missionTurn classroom theory into vehicles that run.
  • Our methodMini-projects for juniors, prototypes for the whole crew.
  • Our communityWorkshops, talks and competitions open to everyone.

Pick your division

You don’t need experience. Choose the field that excites you and start building.

Embedded and electronics

Microcontrollers, CAN and LIN buses, sensors and battery systems.

Mechanical design

CAD, assemblies, gears and finite-element studies.

Simulation and modelling

Vehicle dynamics and ride models in MATLAB and Simulink.

Software, AI and web

Computer vision, dashboards and the club’s web platform.

Questions

Do I need experience to join?

No. Junior members start with a guided mini-project and learn the tools along the way.

Who can apply?

Any ENISo student can apply and choose one or more technical divisions.

How much time does it take?

Plan a few hours a week. Your project leader will set milestones with you.
